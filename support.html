Help & Support

Find help with the current iPhone preview, original illustrated concept stories, and creator-credited open short films.

Updated November 12, 2026 / Covers the current internal 0.1.0 prototype

Current preview, not a paid streaming service. The fictional illustrated concepts are short demos, not acted or licensed commercial drama seasons. The separately labeled Open Films library contains credited creator-origin short films. Some other story links open the creators' own websites; availability is controlled by those publishers.

How to get help

For a bug, playback problem or accessibility concern, open a support request in our public project-support issue tracker. A free GitHub account may be required to post. Current internal TestFlight participants can also use Apple's built-in Send Beta Feedback feature, which can attach an app screenshot or crash report under Apple's controls.

Please include the app's version and build number, iPhone model, iOS version, which screen you used, what you expected, and what happened. Do not post passwords, real account identifiers, personal videos, private URLs or confidential documents publicly.

Video or audio will not play

Check device volume, silent/audio route and internet availability for external publisher pages. Built-in illustrated concept clips and bundled open-film previews normally play locally. Restart the app and try the same title again. If the problem repeats, include the film title and episode number in a support report.

Saved titles or progress look wrong

The preview keeps demo bookmarks and playback progress on your iPhone. In the app's Account/Me screen, review local history and preferences. Use Clear viewing history or Erase all demo data only if you intentionally want to reset saved local records; these controls cannot undo a deletion.

Why are some chapters only 12 seconds?

Those are original illustrated story-concept clips, labeled as demos. They do not include filmed performances, full episode masters or paid viewing rights. The actual creator-credited open films are in a separately labeled section and retain their original film credits. Do not pay or grant permissions to unlock fictional concept chapters.

Privacy, attribution and public release

See the Privacy Policy for local-data handling and outbound links. Creator names, individual film licenses, source links and attribution are displayed with the applicable films in the app. Third-party publishers manage their own websites and streaming rights. This preview has no live subscriptions, payments or paid rewards. Public App Store release has not been announced.
