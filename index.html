Limitless Short Drama · iOS

Privacy Policy

Your viewing choices belong to you. The current iPhone preview combines original illustrated concept clips with separately credited open short films and stores your viewing preferences on your device.

Updated November 12, 2026 · Applies to the current iOS preview, version 0.1.0, including local illustrated concepts and credited open-film screenings

No developer-operated app analytics or viewing-history collection. The current iOS preview stores bookmarks and playback progress locally and does not send these records to us, a developer-run cloud account or in-app advertising analytics. External publisher links and this GitHub Pages website have separate data practices, described below.

What stays on your device

The app stores preferences and demo activity locally using Apple's on-device storage, including saved series, Continue Watching history, episode progress, recent searches, autoplay and captions settings, and Shorts mute preferences. This supports local functionality. The current beta does not send these records to a developer-operated account or server.

What the prototype does not do

The current internal beta has no account registration, ad tracking, production analytics SDK, purchases or subscriptions. It includes original short illustrated fictional-story concepts and separately labeled, locally bundled creator-credited open films whose individual Creative Commons attributions appear in the app. It does not provide rights-managed commercial drama series or in-app paid streaming. Apple separately processes TestFlight invitations, beta feedback, crash reports or device diagnostics under its own terms.

External publisher links and film credits

Some story cards link to a creator's or publisher's own public website using normal iOS browser behavior. Those third-party pages may see your network address and browser activity under their own policies. The app does not claim to license, download or host those external series as paid native episodes. The credited open films packaged with the preview play locally, with source/license links shown in their film details.

Manage and delete your local data

In the app's Account screen, use Clear viewing history to delete local viewing-progress records or Erase all demo data to delete locally saved shows, playback settings, search history and watch progress. You may also remove the app from your device. Operating-system backups and Apple-managed TestFlight data, if any, are governed separately by your Apple settings and Apple's policies.

Sharing and retention

Because this beta does not transmit app activity to us, we do not operate a server-side personal viewing-history database and do not sell or share those records for advertising. The app retains its local demo state until you clear it or uninstall the app.

Changes to this policy

We will update the policy and the App Store privacy disclosures before enabling features that change data handling—such as real accounts, third-party licensed streaming, subscriptions, recommendations or remote analytics. The effective date above identifies this beta's current practices.

Contact

TestFlight participants can use Apple's built-in TestFlight feedback feature. For playback or accessibility help, visit our Support page. For a privacy issue, please open a privacy inquiry. Do not include passwords, private account identifiers or sensitive personal information in a public issue.

About this website

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process website access information under its privacy statement. Hosting this document does not mean the iOS app transmits your viewing data to GitHub.
